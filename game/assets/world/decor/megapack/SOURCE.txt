Summary of that license as it applies here: use in commercial games is
permitted, and so is modifying the art for use inside the game. NOT permitted:
redistributing these files standalone, or including them in another asset
pack. Shipping them compiled into the game build is the permitted form.

All 144 props imported, driven by the pack's own Documentation/ASSET_LIST.json
(name, category, size and anchor per asset). Two transforms on import:

 - CROPPED to the opaque bounding box. The pack ships 16px of transparent
   padding and declares an `anchor` at the foot of the content; DecorMarker
   draws bottom-centred on the click point, so trimming the padding makes a
   prop stand exactly on its anchor instead of floating 16px above it.

 - DOWNSCALED 1/4 (LANCZOS). The pack's art is about 4.7x the scale of the
   rest of the project — its well is 261x279 where the Plains pack's is
   55x61. This is painted illustration, not pixel art, so resampling is safe
   here in a way it would not be for a real pixel-art sheet.

STYLE WARNING, recorded deliberately: this art is painted illustration in a
pronounced isometric 3/4 view, while the rest of the game (Plains props, the
road tiles, the Hypnobius structures) is flat-shaded pixel art seen closer to
top-down. They do not share a look or a camera angle. Imported anyway at the
user's explicit request, after a side-by-side comparison; see
assets/_incoming/COMPARACION_megapack_vs_plains.png.
